What stays consistent

Each row keeps its camera, geometry, object colors, four-step lighting and fog fixed. Only the surface color modulation changes. The lower row uses the existing generated world and meshes.

The close study uses two large slabs to make the differences readable. The variation changes color, not geometry or surface normals. It does not add relief or cast shadows.

What implementation needs

Anchor the pattern to stable world coordinates so it stays attached during flight and coordinate rebasing. Keep the variation broad enough to survive the low-resolution proxy, and check the result through diffusion before tuning its strength.

These stills establish appearance only. Live GPU cost, distant aliasing, and generated-image behavior remain to be checked after choosing a direction.
