FABLE / MOVEMENT STUDY

A route that fades behind you

Turn around to see where you came from. Each part of the trail disappears five seconds after you pass it.

A

Pale filament

One fine line makes the route easy to follow. My pick.

B

Sparse beads

Small points mark the route with more space between them.

C

Dashed ribbon

Short, wider strokes give the route a stronger presence.

All three use the same route, pale color, and five-second lifetime. The oldest section fades first; the newest section lasts longest.

Illustrative mock over an actual generated scene. This is not a live 3D rendering. The trail would stay in the world as you move and turn.
